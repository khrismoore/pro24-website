Roof Repair & Replacement

Repair What You Can. Replace What You Must.

Not every roof problem needs a full replacement. Repair can address localized issues — missing shingles, flashing failures, exposed fasteners, or a leak tied to one specific detail — without disturbing a roof that still has useful service life. Replacement becomes the practical choice when wear is widespread, leaks keep recurring, or repeated repairs no longer restore confidence.

  • A repair addresses the actual entry point — not just the visible symptom
  • Full replacement scope: materials, ventilation, flashing, underlayment, disposal
  • Concealed decking damage is documented and discussed before extra work proceeds
  • Property protected and checked for debris at the end of each work day

After the Storm, An Expert by Your Side

Hail and wind damage are not always obvious from the driveway. Missing or creased shingles, dents in soft metal, displaced siding, and new water marks indoors all deserve a closer look. Pro24 inspects the exterior, documents visible findings with photos and written reports, and helps organize a repair or restoration scope — with 24/7 emergency response and tarping when it can't wait.

  • Hail & wind damage repair, leak repair, full restoration
  • Emergency tarping & temporary protection, available 24/7
  • Detailed documentation and a written scope your adjuster can use
  • Straight talk: your insurance carrier decides coverage — Pro24 explains the construction condition and the work needed to restore the property

Commercial & Multifamily

Working Around Your Business, Not Against It

Commercial and multifamily roofs are built differently than a residential shingle roof. Most are flat or low-slope, which means drainage, membrane condition, and rooftop penetrations do more of the work that pitch and shingles do on a house. Pro24 inspects, repairs, and replaces commercial roofing across the metro — coordinating access, staging, and cleanup with property managers so the work moves forward without disrupting the business underneath it.

Residential

Experienced and prepared to handle any residential roofing, siding, and window replacement needs you may have — starting from a single concern like a ceiling stain or lifted shingle.

Multifamily

Property managers, building owners, and area developers turn to Pro24 for exterior work planned around residents and operations — from apartments to condos and townhomes.

Commercial

Flat and low-slope specialists: membrane surface, seams and flashing, drains and scuppers, equipment curbs, and ponding — inspected and handled around business hours.

Discussing a multifamily or commercial property? Share the location, number of buildings, current concern, and access limitations — call 612-235-8696.
